## Design and complete path

The existing `forecast --file PATH` command and Forecast workspace recognize `raven.forecast-scenarios/v1`. Explicit resource alternatives optionally derive prospective points from a copied W33 queue model with one exact target. Recorded residuals calibrate those points using earlier fit/later calibration halves, complete request/policy context and robust drift checks. Unknown policy/reservations, out-of-distribution requests and sparse history cause explicit abstention. Synthetic outcomes never train the recorded recommendation.

A simpler cohort median remains an audit baseline. A high-dimensional learned predictor would require a larger independent corpus and more opaque assumptions. The selected residual correction is inspectable, bounded and deliberately experimental. Its model identity, input digest, uncertainty and limits travel through existing persistence, worker, renderer and export paths.

Shorter wall-time alternatives require comparable runtime observations and a 95% Wilson upper overrun-risk bound within the declared tolerance. Timeout censoring remains unresolved unless the observed lower bound already proves overrun. Missing/sparse runtime evidence cannot justify a smaller request. The result never changes a scheduler request, submits or cancels work.

At most eight alternatives, 1,000 outcome records, 256 modeled queue jobs and the last 100 audit targets per origin are supported. Each target uses only outcomes completed before its prediction. Simple cohort, current Raven and new residual models report start/wait MAE, tail error, prediction/interval coverage, Brier/log scores and exact denominator counts. Recorded and synthetic audits remain separate. Known hidden state and missing policies are limitations, not simulated ground truth.

## Independent prospective scheduler holdout

The controlled Slurm 26.05.5 fixture captured **65 independently preissued observations**, all with `submit <= issued_at < actual_start < actual_end`; none were refused. Each fixed 2 s model point was fsynced before the owned target was released. The original 420 s driver budget and all parameters stayed unchanged. Full wrapper time including service initialization/cleanup was 421.56 s (00:59:27.505–01:06:29.063 UTC on 2026-10-18); the bounded observation driver completed within its budget. Services and owned jobs were cleaned up. No competing tests/builds ran during capture.

The first 40 outcomes supplied earlier history; **25 later attempts** produced chronological held-out predictions for each model. Overall prediction coverage was **25/65 = 38.46%**, transparently including initial abstention; coverage after the history threshold was 25/25. Every prediction used only already-completed earlier outcomes.

| Model | MAE (s) | p95 absolute error (s) | Interval coverage | Brier score | Log score |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Simple cohort | 0.37580 | 1.98873 | 0.84 | 0.14111 | 0.46585 |
| Existing Raven predictor | 0.36000 | 2.00000 | 0.84 | 0.13870 | 0.45915 |
| Queue residual | 0.37580 | 1.98873 | 0.84 | 0.14111 | 0.46585 |

This is **negative efficacy evidence**: the new model did not improve the existing predictor and matched the cohort baseline. Constant prospective points provide no extra ranking signal beyond that cohort; this fixture validates the real preissue/calibration/audit path, not improved counterfactual prediction. Differences are based on only 25 held-out predictions with second-resolution scheduler outcomes. They do not establish statistical superiority, calibration on production clusters, or causal effects of changed requests. The product decision remains experimental (`production_qualified: false`). No tuning followed this holdout.

`measurements/forecast-temporal-holdout/` retains model, results, every issued point/outcome, image/package identity, source hashes, and compressed original command capture with its uncompressed SHA256. Images reused exact-version compiled packages and the current read-only entrypoint; no clean-current-snapshot rebuild is claimed. A deterministic test replays this actual corpus and preserves its absence of efficacy gain. The forecast/JSON/queue-model sweep passed **65 tests in 2.78 s** after adding that regression.
